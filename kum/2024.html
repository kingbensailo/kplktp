Group Inthen Dan

Kum 2024 hian group thumah then a ni a. Branch Committee Member's atangin Leader, Secretary leh Treasurer tur te ruat nghal an ni a. Kohhran Representative Upa Lallianthanga'n group hming tur atana Isua zirtir sawm pahnih (12) te atanga mi sawm (10) a tlang atangin Kohhran Representative Upa R. Thangbanglova'n pathum a pawt a. Chung a pawh chhuah te atang chuan a hnuai a mi te hi group hming atan an pawm.
